Privacy Policy

Flicker Check  ·  Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

Flicker Check measures flicker with the phone camera. The app has no account. It does not collect or transmit any user data to external servers. All data stays exclusively on the user's device.

Data stored locally

The app saves only the following on your device:

  • the calibration scale for this phone's camera
  • a photo, only if you tap Take photo

This data is used solely to operate the app. It is never shared with the developer or with any other party.

Camera

The camera is used to measure how a light or a screen flickers.

Frames are processed entirely on your device. They are never sent to any server. The measurement kept on the phone is the frequency, the modulation, and the risk badge.

Camera access is required solely for this purpose.

Photos

Take photo saves an image on your phone. The image includes the camera picture and the charts. It leaves the phone only if you send it with the system share sheet, to an app you choose. Flicker Check does not receive that share.

What is not collected

This version does not ask for a name, an email, or contacts. It does not send a device id, a measurement, or a camera frame. It does not use ads or analytics.

Deleting your data

You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app, which permanently removes all saved data.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the app update that needs them.
